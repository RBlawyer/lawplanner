How to study well

Active recall: close the book and write what you remember before checking.

Pomodoro: 25 minutes of focus, 5 minutes of rest. After four rounds, rest 20.

Cases: practise by applying a rule to a fact pattern, not only by rereading it.

Sleep: a full night before an exam beats a late night of cramming.
